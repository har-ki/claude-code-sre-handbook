## Summary

On 2026-06-18, the ecommerce-api `POST /checkout` endpoint began returning HTTP 409 errors at a 50% rate due to a TOCTOU (Time-of-Check-to-Time-of-Use) race condition in `reserveInventory()`. A 50–150ms artificial delay between the stock check and the inventory decrement allowed concurrent requests to read stale stock, all pass the check, and all decrement — driving Product 7 (Ceramic Plant Pot) to -3 units. Once negative, all subsequent requests for that product failed immediately, and the error rate stabilized at 50% as a stuck state with no self-recovery.

## Root cause

`reserveInventory()` in `otel-demo/ecommerce/backend/src/services/inventory.js` performed a non-atomic check-then-act sequence: it read the current stock (line 39), checked sufficiency (line 42), then waited 50–150ms for "business rules validation" (line 53), then decremented (line 55). Concurrent requests all read the same pre-decrement stock value, all passed the check, all waited, then all decremented. Product 7 started with 5 units and received at least 4 concurrent 2-unit reservations: `5→3→1→-1→-3`. The in-memory counter is never replenished, so it remained at -3 and every subsequent request failed immediately.

## Contributing factors

- The artificial 50–150ms delay (simulating business rules) was placed between the stock check and the decrement, creating a large race window.
- The in-memory inventory store has no atomic compare-and-swap primitive, so concurrent Node.js async operations interleave freely.
- A post-decrement negative-stock guard (`StockMismatchError`) existed at lines 58–84 but was designed to detect the race after the fact, not prevent it — and once the counter goes negative it stays negative.
- Product 7 had artificially low initial stock (5 units vs. 50 for all other products), making it the first to deplete and surface the bug.

## Detection

Automated alert watcher detected error rate at 50% (threshold: 25%) on a 1-minute lookback window of 68 samples. Fingerprint `ecommerce-api|Error` triggered at 2026-06-18T08:13:23Z.

## Fix

Moved `inventory[item.id] -= item.quantity` and the subsequent negative-stock guard from after the async delay to immediately after the stock check (before the delay). This ensures the reservation is committed synchronously before any async operation releases the event loop, eliminating the TOCTOU window. The artificial delay remains in place after the decrement for any downstream validation that doesn't require holding the stock reservation.

See: `otel-demo/ecommerce/backend/src/services/inventory.js`, PR #19.

## Action items
