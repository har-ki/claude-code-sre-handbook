## Summary

The ecommerce-api checkout endpoint experienced a 20% error rate starting seconds after pod startup on 2026-06-18. All errors were `"Insufficient stock for product 7 (Ceramic Plant Pot)"` caused by a TOCTOU (time-of-check-to-time-of-use) race condition in `inventory.js`. Under concurrent load, multiple requests read the same stock value, all passed the stock check, then each decremented the in-memory inventory — driving it to -3. Every subsequent checkout for product 7 then failed immediately, making the error permanent until pod restart.

## Root cause

`inventory.js` `reserveInventory` read stock via an async function (`getStock`, 5ms delay), checked if stock was sufficient, then awaited a 50–150ms "business rules" delay before decrementing the in-memory store. Because Node.js yields between the check and the write, concurrent requests all read the same stock value (5 for product 7), all passed the check, and all decremented — driving the store to -3. The negative-stock guard on line 58 threw an error but did not restore the inventory, making product 7 permanently unavailable until pod restart.

## Contributing factors

- The 50–150ms async delay between the stock check and decrement greatly widened the TOCTOU window, allowing many concurrent requests to interleave.
- Product 7 (Ceramic Plant Pot) had only 5 units, making it easy to exhaust under modest concurrency.
- The in-memory inventory store was not restored on `StockMismatchError`, causing a permanent error state for the affected product.
- Trace-based alerting was blind to this incident — no spans recorded `StatusCode='Error'` despite 324 confirmed log errors.

## Detection

Detected by the automated log-based alert watcher (fingerprint `ecommerce-api|Error`) when the 1-minute error rate exceeded the 15% threshold. Error rate at trigger: 20% (324/1,600 log entries in the 15-minute window).

## Fix

Replaced the async `getStock(item.id)` call with a direct synchronous read of `inventory[item.id]`, and moved the decrement to immediately after the stock check — before any `await`. Since Node.js is single-threaded, a synchronous check-and-decrement sequence cannot be interleaved by concurrent requests, eliminating the TOCTOU race.

File changed: `otel-demo/ecommerce/backend/src/services/inventory.js`

## Action items

- [ ] Replace the in-memory inventory store with a persistent store (Redis or DB) with atomic compare-and-swap for production resilience.
- [ ] Restore inventory on `StockMismatchError` to prevent permanent product unavailability if a race slips through.
- [ ] Investigate why `span.setStatus(ERROR)` in `checkout.js` does not propagate to stored trace spans — trace-based alerting was blind to this incident.

## Open questions

- Should the artificial 50–150ms "business rules" delay be retained as a load-testing aid or removed to reduce future TOCTOU risk from real async work?
